Pangea Earth combines a scientific plate reconstruction with an interactive globe. The six snapshots show continental positions from 300 million years ago to the present.

What is real?

The continental outlines and location shortcuts come from the Müller et al. (2022) plate motion model, reconstructed through GPlates. These are rotated modern coastline shapes, not a reconstruction of ancient shorelines or sea level. Different models can give different positions.

What is illustrative?

The ancient surface is a procedurally shaded illustration. Its colors, ridges, and fine detail are not measured ancient terrain, vegetation, or satellite imagery. Zooming reveals the illustration in more detail, not additional geological evidence. The globe has a smooth surface, with no reconstructed elevation.

Can I see satellite imagery?

Choose Satellite today to see NASA’s Blue Marble imagery of modern Earth. These satellite-derived composites have a native resolution of about 500 meters. They cannot show Pangea: satellites did not exist 240 million years ago.

Controls

Drag or swipe to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag to tilt. Use + / − for accessible zoom, N to face north, and ◎ to return to the whole planet.
